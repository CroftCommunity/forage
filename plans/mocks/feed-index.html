Open decisions

  1. The provenance line under every feed row (“Bluesky lists it · in the index · in 3 jumpstarts”). It is the honesty the plan asked for (D-own: a reader always knows whose editorial act they see) and it costs a line per row on a phone. Keep as a line, fold into the byline, or show only when it is not the common case?
  2. “likes loading…” on index rows before hydration. D2 says the file carries no count, so the first paint has none. Alternative: show the band as words (“100+ likes”) until the real count lands. The band is already how the Popular sort orders them.
  3. Popular jumpstarts first in the rail by default. Owner’s ask (2026-09-04) with “trending” corrected to “popular” (D6). The rail on a phone is a drawer; is eight rows the right count there, or four?
  4. The jumpstart head links out for the follow (D-follow: no follow-all here). The line under the link says Forage does not change your follows. Is that the right place for that sentence, or should it wait until the button exists?

What the frames hold, and what they cannot

  • The index in the frames is the one the branch commits (data/feed-index.json, built 2026-09-09) — not a fixture. A later regenerate changes the rows; the manifest names the tree.
  • Hydration is fixtured (__echoFeeds: every index row answers with 12,345 likes), so the “likes loading…” state and the settled state are both real paints of the engine, not drawings.
  • The Current frames for /jumpstarts and /j are the route’s absence on main. That is the honest comparison: the surface is new.
  • Not shown: the Discovery index section under Advanced (Add / Replace / Off, paste or pick a file). It is a settings form; the plan’s workflow covers its behaviour and a frame of it adds nothing a form does not already say.
