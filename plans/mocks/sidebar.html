Two answers to “what does the front page show?”

Same sidebar in both. The only difference is where / lands. Everything in the nav is a board — a list of posts — and boards differ only in where the posts come from: a feed generator, a hashtag, or your own graph at some reach. On the phone, tap ☰.

On the phone the nav is a drawer: it costs nothing until you open it, where the two-half strip always cost a row. That is a better answer to “usable but not intrusive” than the strip ever had.

1 · Directory

What you described: trending, then topics, then a feed’s posts. You arrive somewhere neutral and choose. Good for a guest, who has no history worth remembering — and identical signed in or out, so there is one front page to design.

2 · Default board

You land on the board you were last reading, marked in the nav. Good for a regular — no click between opening the app and reading. The directory still exists, at /feeds, where it already lives today.

Why not both

They can be: directory for a guest, last board for a regular. That costs one rule and one stored value, and it is what Reddit does. Flip Session on either landing to see the pair.
