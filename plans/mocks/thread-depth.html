A conversation is not a tree

Three proposals and four decisions, all settled, from the owner’s report on 2026-09-02 with a screenshot of a live thread seven levels deep: “we have deep nested threads not collapsed by default … it can get a little hard to follow and read. can you research how reddit handled this and propose a solution? maybe collapse at n depth or n responses by default?”

The two proposals answer different halves of that, and the frames are here so you can see whether you want one, both, or neither. Phase A buys back the width the indent was spending. Phase B bounds the scroll. Phase A hides nothing and costs the reader no taps; Phase B is the “collapse at n depth” the report asked about, and it is the one with a cost.

v3 — every decision is answered, and decision 2 grew a third phase. The owner, 2026-09-03: “let's say no to the tildes and everything else looks good for A+B, but I think the settings should be adjustable for the user in advanced on their profile.” So: no label (decision 3 closes on A, and the two drawn alternatives stay below as the record of what was compared), and Phase C — both numbers become reader settings under Advanced on /me, which is decision 2 answered by handing it over rather than by picking. Decisions 1 and 4 were settled in v2: both phases are wanted, and the find-in-page cost is accepted.

Every frame here is a capture of the engine (MOCKS.md P1). Current is main at e6c5fa8; Phase A is claude/thread-depth at bf16319; Phase A+B+C is the same branch at b493be1. All three come from scripts/mock-snaps.mjs against the same population, in the default skin (rule 4), at 390×844 and 1280×900. Captures live in plans/mocks/snaps/thread-depth/ (rule 3). Nothing on this page is drawn.

The population is new and it is measured (P2): e2e/harness/mock-deepthread.mjs. The words are written, not copied.

What Reddit actually does

Reddit does not collapse by depth — it truncates and re-roots

  • Past roughly nine or ten levels, old.reddit stops rendering and emits continue this thread →, a link that reloads the page with that comment as the new root. It is a render budget, not a fold. Forage already has exactly this, at depth 10 (js/substrates/lens.js) — it is visible in the Current frames below.
  • Siblings are budgeted separately: load more comments (N replies) after a per-level batch. Forage has this too (CHILD_PAGE = 20).
  • The apps cap the visual indent. Past about five levels the offset stops growing and a coloured rail carries the parentage instead. This is the part forage does not have, and it is what Phase A is.
  • The only real collapse-by-default on Reddit is score-driven (“comment score below threshold”). That mechanism is a downvote feature, and forage retired downvotes on 2026-08-27. Neither proposal here revives it: that decision was about a judgement of a comment; these are facts about the tree.
  • Tildes ran Phase A as an experiment in 2018 and kept it: flatten at depth 4+ when a comment has exactly one reply. Theirs labels the flattened reply “(Reply to above comment)” in words; ours relies on the unbroken rail — decision 3 below.
The diagnosis

Most of those indents were saying nothing

An indent is the renderer saying “this branched”. In the owner’s screenshot, and in the spine of this fixture, nothing ever branched — each reply was the only reply. The indent was spent and nothing was bought with it:

On a desktop the same staircase costs no readability but leaves a growing empty gutter down the left of the column, which is what makes a long exchange hard to follow: the eye has to track a diagonal.

Decision 3, drawn · phone · 390×844 · the run, fully open

Does a flattened reply need to say so in words? — no

Decided 2026-09-03: A. Owner: “let's say no to the tildes”. The frames stay as the record of what was compared; neither label is on the branch. All three are the same thread, every fold pressed open, anchored at depth 2 — the rung the flattening starts under, so the whole run is in frame. Six replies, each the only reply to the one above it. Only the label differs.

A — nothing (as built) forage@b493be1
B — Tildes, every rung forage@085c015
C — once per run forage@ce62744
  • B is the honest version of the Tildes design and it is also its own argument against itself: five identical parentheticals down one chain, each pushing the reply’s first words onto another line. On a 390px phone that is roughly a full extra line of height per rung, spent saying the same thing six times.
  • C says it once, at the top of the run, and lets the unbroken rail carry it after that — which is what the rail is for. A run can start at any depth (a branch interrupts one and the next chain begins a new one), so “first of a run” is structural, not a count: the reply’s parent is a chain deep enough to flatten and the grandparent is not.
  • A is what is built. The case for it is that B and C are both annotating something the reader can already see — the rail runs from one avatar into the next without a step, and nothing else in forage draws that shape.
  • The desktop frames differ by one rung, not by kind. The desktop run starts at depth 5 rather than depth 3, so B and C sit one level lower; the comparison is the same and it is not repeated here.

Building it caught a bug that looking at it did not. Variant C first shipped its desktop and phone selectors both unfenced. The flatten rules can do that — the phone set is a superset of the desktop set, so both firing is the same answer twice — but C’s is not monotonic: at 390px the desktop selector labelled depth 5 (its run start, counted from a depth-4 threshold) while the phone selector labelled depth 3, and the variant whose whole claim is “once per run” printed it twice. The frame we had was anchored at depth 5, showed one label, and looked right. It took reading computed ::before content at both widths to see it — and a new frame, deep-lens-run, that starts where the run does.

Phase C · the numbers become the reader’s

Two dials under Advanced, and an off switch that means it

Decision 2 was an argument about two numbers. Phase C answers it by handing them over. Stop indenting a chain of single replies is a width question, which is why Auto is a real answer and the default — a phone and a desktop each keep their own threshold. Fold replies deeper than is a scroll question, the same at every width, so there is nothing for auto to mean there.

Advanced, Proposed forage@b493be1
Advanced, Current forage@e6c5fa8
Both turned off forage@b493be1
  • Off is main’s thread, exactly — every indent kept, nothing folded. That is the third frame, and it is what makes these settings rather than a milder version of our opinion. It is a claim in the gate, not a hope: the workflow reads the depths that flatten and the depths that fold off the rendered DOM and asserts both lists are empty.
  • The choices. Indent: Auto · after level 1 · 2 · 4 · 6 · never. Fold: deeper than 3 · 5 · 8 · never.
  • Device-local, like the skin and the card size, and never account state. A corrupt stored value reads as no choice and says so once in the console; the default is written onto the root explicitly, so no stylesheet rule has to guess what “unset” means.
  • Where each number lives is not arbitrary. The stylesheet owns the indent thresholds (one selector per choice, keyed on data-flatten) because indenting is a style question and CSS is what knows the screen width. The renderer owns the fold, read at render time, because folding is a decision about what to draw and CSS cannot count.

The panel’s first capture caught its own layout bug: .stack is a margin rule and not a column, so the short label sat beside its select while the long one wrapped above its own. Fixed in b493be1.

Evidence

What is gated, and what is not

  • e2e/mock-depth.workflow.mjs — nine claims, red against main at each phase’s centre (no .chain class; depth 2 indents, 67 vs 53; nothing folds at depth 5) and green on the branch. It holds: a chain stops indenting and a branch does not; the deepest reply keeps 270px of a 390px phone where main measures 158px; nothing shallower than the budget folds; the bar names the whole subtree, meets the 44px tap floor, and one press opens the branch; and a ?focus= permalink into a folded subtree still lands.
  • e2e/mock-thread.workflow.mjs — still green. Phase A carried a CSS repair to the elbow geometry (a child’s elbow read --kids-indent, which resolves on the child, and only ever agreed with the parent’s padding because every comment carried the same number), and that workflow measures elbow against rail at both widths.
  • Gates on the branch: npm test 723/723, npm run workflows 38 found / 0 failed, npm run conformance.
  • e2e/mock-depth.workflow.mjs — nine more claims for Phase C, red against main at the first (main writes no data-flatten). They read the shape off the rendered DOM under four settings pairs, never off the module that wrote them: auto/5 flattens from level 2 and folds at 5; off/off flattens and folds nothing; 1/3 and 6/8 land where they say. The dials carry the stored choice, meet the 44px tap floor, take effect with no reload, and survive one.
  • Not gated: the four decisions. Three are judgements about numbers or words, and the fourth (find-in-page) is a browser behaviour no test of ours can hold.
  • Found, filed, not fixed: the focus bar reads “That comment isn’t in this thread” over a comment it did find, unfold and highlight, whenever the target arrives with the quote cascade.
