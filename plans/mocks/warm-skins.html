Forage — the warm set: four skin families, each with a lighter and a darker side

Mock v1 · Current frames forage@fe2b77b (main, the tree the owner runs) · Proposed frames forage@35b64db (branch claude/warm-skins) · every frame is a capture of the engine by scripts/mock-snaps.mjs over the hermetic lens:mock-board / lens:mock-thread populations, signed out, at 390×844 and 1280×900 · manifest: snaps/warm-skins/manifest.json

What this shows. Eight new skins in four families — Rosewater, Lavender, Apricot, Seaglass — proposed for Settings → Skin, beside the four families forage ships today (Forage, Classic BBS, Usenet gray, phpBB), all of which are cool or neutral. The ask (owner, 2026-08-30): "more warm or feminine energy … look at some old popular phpbb skins with some diversity to reimplement an inspired version from, each should have a lighter and a darker mode." Each family is inspired by a colour variant the classic phpBB era actually ran, keeps prosilver’s grammar (filled band, pale tab strip, white cards on a tinted ground, striped rows, rounded corners) and none of its hexes — every value is ours, and every text pair clears AA (test/skins.test.js recomputes 24 role pairs per skin).

Read this before comparing. MOCKS.md says compare in ONE skin, because the skin is usually noise. Here the skin is the subject, so every Proposed frame is deliberately in a different skin, and each frame’s caption and the manifest say which. Nothing structural differs between any two frames — a skin restyles and restructures nothing — so any layout difference you think you see is the same tree in different clothes. The pictures in the board fixture point at a test CDN and never load; the empty stages are the fixture, not the skin.
