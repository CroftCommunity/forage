View modes — clip and gram, a board shown one post per screen Proposed is a sketch

Three proposals, nine frames. v2 after the owner's reading of v1 (2026-09-16): the reel is a mode — clip (video-centred) beside a gram (image-centred), any mode at any ring stop — and the control is a second pill under the ring pill. Current is a capture of the engine: be82a95 on claude/video-view, whose UI tree is main at c3a4abe exactly (the branch adds a plan file and nothing else), captured by scripts/mock-snaps.mjs against lens:mock-board — the board-cards fixture that carries a native clip, a quoted clip, a YouTube card and a portrait picture — in the default skin, at 390×844 and 1280×900 (MOCKS.md). Proposed is a SKETCH, drawn in this page's own CSS with the fixture's own content (the clip by tiredactor.bsky.social, its words and its counts), because nothing is built: js/ui/reel.js does not exist. Nothing here is approvable as pixels; the sketch is a direction to talk about, and Phase 4 of the plan replaces every Proposed frame with a capture of the engine before any decision is called locked.

Open decisions (the plan's D1–D6, as this sketch reads them)

  1. D1 — where the clips come from at a people-scope. The sketch's count line reads clips from 701 people you follow: it sources from the scope's members with the video-only author feed, not from Home's feeds narrowed. The strict reading would say clips in Home by people you follow and show one clip per page.
  2. D2 — the address. Drawn as /m/home?view=clips, with a Rows control on the strip that returns to /m/home at the same scroll position. Alternative: a route of its own.
  3. D3 — autoplay. Drawn ON by default inside Clips (muted, in view only), with the second frame showing the OFF state. The GIF autoplay setting is not reused; a clip is a video post.
  4. D4 — order. Drawn with the deal (round-robin across people or sources). The strip's 2 of 9 is the loaded window, not a total — the reel says what it has, as a board's window sort does.
  5. D5 — a clip-posters index. Not drawn. World's reel here is the composition only.
  6. D6 — the word. Decided on v1 (owner, 2026-09-16: "we are looking at a 'mode' here like 'clip' mode that is vids centered, and we could do a 'gram' mode where it could be images centred"). Rows · Clip · Gram.
  7. D7 — where the control lives. Drawn as the owner suggested: a mode pill under the ring pill in the left nav ("any mode could be used at any ring scale. Maybe we put a mode slider under the ring slider?"). The open half: on a phone the nav is a drawer, so the strip keeps Rows as the way back in the sketch — is that wanted, or is the drawer enough?
  8. D8 — the word in code. js/mode.js already means which population the app is (Bluesky view / memory sandbox). Proposal: readers say mode; the code and the key say view (forage.view, ?view=clip).
  9. D9 — is a GIF a gram? Proposal: gram shows GIFs paused with their badge; clip does not show them.
  10. D10 — alt text in gram. Drawn visible (the picture is the screen; the words a person wrote about it are the caption). Rows keep the hidden default.

What the sketch claims, and what only a capture can

  • Same posts, same policy. The reel is the board's own posts narrowed to media.kind === 'video', after blocks, mutes, labels and the ring — the order the shape layer already applies. Nothing on this page needs a new write, a new host, or a lexicon.
  • The count line is the honest one. 2 of 9 loaded is the loaded window; at a people-scope the sentence names the source (clips from 701 people you follow) so D1's difference from rows is visible on the surface, not buried.
  • The fan-out is bounded by the reader, not a cap. The next wave of author feeds is asked for when the reel is within a few clips of its end. Whether that holds at fol (701 follows) and at hop is Phase 0's number, and no drawing can show it — which is exactly why this is a sketch.
  • Not drawn: the empty reel (no clips in this board's loaded posts — More?), the hashtag reel signed out (a worded refusal, as /h/ boards give), reduced motion (no autoplay; the frames are otherwise identical), the memory population (an honest empty page — a frontier row).
  • v2 reframing: one renderer, parameterised by mode; the kind filter is media.kind, already on every shaped post. Gram adds no player and no new fetch shape — at a people-scope it is the same fan-out with filter=posts_with_media (lexicon-verified; its yield is Phase 0f).
  • Mapping to the plan: the mode pill → Phase 2 (js/ui/nav.js, js/view-mode.js); the strip and the switch → Phase 2 (js/ui/lens-views.js); the stack, the observer, the labeled-clip rule, the sound control → Phase 2 (js/ui/reel.js); the count line's source sentence → Phase 1 (lens.clips()); the frames' promises → Phase 3 (e2e/clips.workflow.mjs); these sketches → replaced by captures in Phase 4.
