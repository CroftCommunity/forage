Forage · mock · ring-scope

The ring becomes a scope, and one pill sets it

Sixteen frames, six surfaces, phone and desktop. Captures of the engine, not drawings: sections A–D are Current forage@8bc6cbd (main) beside Proposed forage@f52b8a6 (claude/ring-scope, landed #55); section E, added 2026-09-04, is Current forage@8b249bf (main, the ring already landed) beside Proposed forage@016b1d8 (claude/ring-universe). All by scripts/mock-snaps.mjs at 390×844 and 1280×900 against the same population. What this mock is for: the ring stops being five places you go and becomes one control over what every surface shows you — and, in E, what "inside the ring" means on a thread.

Read the pill as a scope, not a destination. On main, "Your ring" is five rows in the left nav and pressing one takes you to a board of those people. On the branch the section keeps its place and holds one control: it takes you nowhere, and it decides who is in everything you are already looking at. World is the default, so a reader who never touches it sees exactly what they see today.

B · the thread

B. A thread carries its own pill, and it overrides that thread only

The ring applies site-wide, threads included — the owner's call, against this plan's own recommendation. That leaves one real hazard: a reply your ring hid is a reply whose answer you cannot see. So the thread grew a widening control rather than an exemption. Moving it changes this thread; the pill in the nav stays where you left it, and navigating away drops the override. An earlier draft of this mock showed two identical pills stacked here — the site's under the masthead and the thread's directly below it. Moving the site-wide pill into the nav is what fixed that: on a phone this is now the only pill on screen.

C · signed out

C. A guest sees the pill, locked to World

Owner's call, and a deliberate exception to the guest-surface rule that hides what a reader cannot use. The rule's own reasoning is what allows it: it exists so a control does not sit there absorbing clicks, and a locked segment absorbs nothing while showing a signed-out reader that scoping exists and where they stand. Mutuals and Follows are drawn and dimmed, each with a tooltip saying they are computed from your own follow graph; World is selected and live. The five rows could never have done this — five rows minus the four you cannot use is a list of one, which reads as broken.

D · settings

D. Advanced: which stops the pill offers, and whether feeds are in scope

Composability is the owner's ask — "users can remove or add entries on the slider" — and this is where it is spent. One checkbox per scope, World checked and disabled because it is the way back. Below it, the exemption: Exclude feeds and hashtags from your ring, checked by default, because a feed is something you went and asked for by name. Uncheck it and a quiet feed can render empty, which is the setting working.

E · the thread, inside the ring (2026-09-04)

E. The ring is the whole universe on a thread, with one hole, and the pill says which stops are empty

Owner, on forage.fyi at Follows over a Bluesky Team post, the day after A–D landed: the thread looked "like the posts were deleted". It was the quote cascade — a reply from outside the ring was dropped, a quote from outside it was drawn as a [removed] stub, once per stranger. The Current frame below is that bug under the mock's load: the reader follows the root's author and nobody else, and the one quoter is a stranger. Three things change on the branch. The stub is gone (absent, like a reply). The punch-hole: a post from someone in your ring shows every reply and quote, whoever wrote them — so the head reads 5 replies where main read 1 reply — because "there are only mutuals and mutuals' activities … with this one kind of other punch hole just for practicality"; it is a setting, on by default, and off it the head stands alone. Mutuals is greyed on the pill: nobody on this thread is one ("otherwise what's the point?"), with the reason on hover; Follows stays live because the root's author is one, and World is never muted. The feeds-and-hashtags exemption is unchanged and re-affirmed — "if I go directly to a feed I'm looking to see that feed unedited"; the surface that scopes by default is a future one (mixes, roadmap E159).

Decisions

What was decided, and what is still open

#QuestionAnswer
1What is the middle stop? "Mutuals + follows" computes to the follows rung, so two stops would have shown identical content. Closed — 1a. Mutuals · Follows · World, tightest first. hop stays in the registry for anyone who adds it.
2Does the ring reach inside a thread? Closed — yes, against this plan's recommendation. Decision 4 is what makes that safe.
3Where does the pill live? Closed — the left nav, at the top, where the section already was. An earlier build put it under the masthead; that broke a pinned 320px measurement AND drew two pills on a thread.
4The thread pill — global or local? Closed — a transient override. An argument, never a write, so leaving the thread drops it by construction.
5What does a signed-out reader see? Closed — the pill, locked to World. The one exception to the guest-surface rule, pinned three ways so it cannot rot into "five greyed rows".
6Do the /r/<rung> routes retire? Closed — retired, with the merged board behind them (#58, 2026-09-03).
7(2026-09-04) Inside the ring, does a post from someone in it show its strangers' replies? Closed — yes, by default, as a setting. The punch-hole opens the whole thread under an in-ring root; a root outside the ring stays hidden; blocks and mutes still apply. Whole thread, not direct replies only: a stranger shown while the stranger they answer is absent is the deleted-post look again.
8(2026-09-04) Does the feeds-and-hashtags exemption survive "there is nothing outside the ring"? Closed — yes, unchanged. A feed opened by name is that feed, unedited. The surface that scopes by default is a future one: mixes (roadmap E159).
9(2026-09-04) Does the thread pill offer a stop nobody on the thread belongs to? Closed — greyed, reason on hover. Never World, never the current stop. The members walk lands after the paint and swaps the bar, so the thread and its cascade never wait for it.
What the frames cannot show
  • World is unringed, not unfiltered. Blocks, mutes, muted words and label prefs apply identically at every stop. The ring is the last of four policies and can only ever remove — it never gives back what an earlier one took.
  • Your own posts are in every ring. Every rung is a cumulative union starting at me, so no setting can narrow you out of your own conversation.
  • An honestly empty board is the feature. Scoped to Follows with quiet follows, a board renders empty and says so. That is the ring working, not a fetch failing.
  • Nothing is badged. A post outside your ring is absent, not greyed with a reason — the same rule mutes already follow, because a row announcing what it withheld defeats the withholding twice.
