Posts, threads, and the ⋯ menu

Six proposals (A–F) and ten open decisions, made to be talked through — not code. The purpose: make a Forage thread read the way the Reddit and Bluesky screenshots in ~/Downloads/new/Photos-1-001 read — who said what, what answers what, what is a repost — with fewer things to tap by accident on a phone. A is the header line, B the ⋯ menu, C the threading, D the like control, E the sort control, F deep links to comments. Every proposal shows Forage today beside the proposal; the “today” frames in C are also captured pixels, not drawings (see snaps/).

Proposal A

The header line: avatar · name · time, and ⋯ in the corner

Every post and every comment opens the same way: a 32px avatar, the author, a dot, how long ago. On a feed row the board (f/gardening) leads the line instead, because that is what you scan a mixed feed by. The ⋯ sits top-right on the feed row and on the read view, so it is one gesture in both places. Vote moves off the left rail and into the action row (Proposal D), which is what frees the left column for the avatar. The lettered circles here stand in for the account's real picture — the lens already carries avatar on profiles and feeds; posts and comments would render the same URL, and the masthead's initials stand-in becomes the loading state only (decision 8).

Proposal B

The ⋯ menu: Bluesky's list, Forage's items

Bluesky's shape: one flat list, label left, icon right, destructive last. The thin separators are the only grouping — three groups (post · thread · account), no headings and no submenus. Open it above: a popover under the ⋯ on desktop, a bottom sheet on the phone (Reddit's “Options” sheet, with Bluesky's rows). What goes in it is the decision; the list here is a starting proposal.

Post

  • Copy post text — as on Bluesky.
  • Copy link — the shareable /p?uri= form (3v already builds it for board rows).
  • Open on bsky.app — the lens is a lens; say so with a door.
  • Save — piggybacks on Bluesky's own: app.bsky.bookmark.createBookmark / deleteBookmark / getBookmarks, which are private, server-side XRPC with no record (docs/LEXICON-REGISTER.md § bookmarks). One intent, one outcome: a save here is the same save as on bsky.app and stays as private as theirs. The public community.lexicon.bookmarks.bookmark record is the one to avoid for exactly that reason. Memory mode keeps the local store. Verify the three procedures against the live lexicon before building — the register names them, no code calls them yet.

Thread

  • Mute thread · Mute words & tags — real atproto preferences, and the second is the doorway to the hashtag-prefs work (plan 2026-08-28-2).
  • Hide for me — local, reversible.

Account · then the red one

  • Mute account · Block account — both are records on Bluesky; the copy should say which one is visible to them.
  • Report post — last, and the only red item. Today report is a prompt(); it wants a sheet of its own.
  • Steward items (remove / approve / pin / lock) appear as a fourth group only when you can moderate — never a second menu.
  • Delete replaces Report when the post is yours (phase 2 two-click arming stays).

Not in the menu: Translate (Bluesky has it; we have no translator) and Award (Reddit's). Like, reply and repost stay in the action row because they are the things you do to most posts; the menu is for the things you do to few.

Proposal C

Threading: a rail only where there is a branch

Today every comment carries the collapse gutter — a direct reply to the post looks “nested” under nothing, and on a phone the whole left edge is a hit target (the second row below is today, drawn with the same content). Reddit's model reads correctly: a top-level comment sits flush; a connector is drawn only from a comment to its own replies, elbowing into each child's avatar. The connector is paint, not a button. Collapse is a single ⊖ on the actions row; the count of hidden replies lives on the ⊕ that replaces it. A repost-with-content (quote) keeps its wall — it is a different thing — but it gets the same avatar header as everything else, loses its tint on the phone, and only the explicit “open its thread ↳” is a link.

What changes in the grammar

  • Depth 0 has no rail. The rail says “these answer the comment above”, so it starts under a comment, never under the post.
  • The rail is paint. pointer-events:none; nothing on the left edge is tappable. The current 24px gutter button is what makes a scroll on a phone open or close threads by accident.
  • Collapse is ⊖ on the actions row, and only on comments that have children. Collapsed, it becomes ⊕ and says how many it is hiding — the note the gutter writes today, moved onto the control.
  • The elbow lands on the child's avatar, which is why avatars in comments earn their 32px: they are the anchors the connector points at.
  • Quotes keep the wall, lose the box. The brand-green wall stays the grammar for quoted material (3q) but moves to the node's outer edge, left of the avatar — so it reads as "this whole entry is a different kind of thing", not as a box inside a comment. The tint goes everywhere.
  • Every node has ⋯ top-right, exactly where the post has it: Copy text · Copy link · Open on bsky.app / Hide for me / Mute account · Block account / Report — hide a reply, or mute or block its author, from the comment or the quote. Try briar's.
  • A quote gets the full row — Reply, Repost, share, with its like under the avatar like any comment. You can reply to the quote or repost it; both are valid, and today's node offers neither. The old “open its thread ↳” link is gone: the ⋯ already has “Open on bsky.app”, and the quote's own Forage thread is one tap on its text (F).
  • Two icons, two verbs. The speech bubble means "open the comments" (a count); Reply is a return arrow. Reddit uses the bubble for both.

Trade-offs to talk through

  • Losing the gutter loses the “click anywhere on the left to fold” affordance that the spec (§3.3) called the signature. The proposal bets that it was never discoverable on a phone and cost more mis-taps than it saved.
  • Reddit fades the connector after the last child; here the last elbow is masked with the card colour, which breaks on a striped row background (--row-odd/even). The thread card is unstriped today, so this holds — worth a test.
  • Should a plain reply carry Repost too? On Bluesky a reply is a post and can be reposted; the row here shows it only on quotes, per the ask. Cheap to add, busier row.
  • Deep threads: the connector nests 22px per level (16px on phone). Past depth 5 or so it wants the existing “continue this thread” stub sooner than 10.

Proposal D

The like control: one target, arrow and count together — and it buzzes

On a post, Reddit's pill (arrow · count in one 44px target) replaces our detached ▲ over a number; on a comment, the outlined arrow with the count beside it and Reply spelled out, as in the screenshots. Pressed state is the brand green with the arrow filled, not just a colour change on a 13px glyph. Try the buttons above.

Sizing

  • Post pill: 36px tall on desktop, 44px on phone. On a comment the like sits in the avatar column, on the action row’s line — the arrow level with Reply and ⟳, the count above it — which gives the row back to ⊖ · Reply · ⟳ · share and lines every vote up on one vertical (owner, v7–v8).
  • Tabular numerals so 9 → 10 does not shift the row.
  • Read-only viewer (signed out): the pill renders as a count with no arrow — the same rule voteBox keeps today (owner, 2026-08-27).
  • One implementation. miniVote is the second copy of voteBox; the plan that removed downvotes named “fix one and ship” as the likely mistake. This is the moment to fold them.

Haptics, on by default

  • navigator.vibrate(12) on a like or a promote; nothing on un-like. Optimistic — fires with the flip, not the server round-trip.
  • Default on, one switch in settings (“Buzz on like”), stored like skin and mode.
  • Honest limit: iOS Safari has no vibrate API, and Chrome ignores it until the page has had a user gesture. It degrades to nothing — never a fallback sound, never a toast.
  • Respects prefers-reduced-motion? Open question — it is not motion, but some people set it for exactly this.

Where it lands in code

  • js/ui/components.js — voteBox, miniVote, postRow, commentNode.
  • js/ui/lens-views.js — kindContext, quoteNode, lensNode, the thread head near line 2150.
  • css/app.css — .postrow grid, .gutter, .quote-node, .cvote; six skins style .vote.boost and would need the new class names.
  • Gates that will notice: e2e/no-downvote.workflow.mjs, e2e/density.workflow.mjs, scenarios/comment-tree-collapse.js, the a11y/skins workflows.
Proposal E

Sort: one control bar, on boards and threads — and what Best means

Today there are two mechanisms: the memory tier draws tabs (Hot · New · Top · Rising on a board, Best · Top · New on a thread, in views.js), and the lens draws a toolbar of selects (Feed order · New · Top, a timeframe under Top, density, preview size — boardToolbar in lens-views.js). The proposal adapts the toolbar into one bar used everywhere, including the thread page, and puts every sort on every board. Reddit withholds sorts on new posts and threads; we don't have to.

The bar

  • Two pills: Sort and From (the timeframe), each a select dressed as the action-row pill so the page has one vocabulary. From shows only when the sort is Top or Best.
  • Same bar on a board and on a thread; on the lens board it keeps density and preview size at its right end, as now.
  • Not the retired two-half strip: that control had two jobs and could not have one (owner, 2026-08-28, nav.js). A select has one.

What Best could mean

  • Likes against replies — a reply that drew answers is engaged with; Wilson on likes/(likes+replies) gives Best a denominator again.
  • Likes with decay — Hot applied to comments: recent-and-liked over old-and-liked. Cheap; Reddit's threads effectively do this.
  • Retire the word — threads default to Top and say so. Honest, and one sort fewer to explain.

Timeframes, friendlier than Reddit

  • Every sort available on every board and thread, whatever its age — no “this post is too new to sort”.
  • Default From: Today on a board, All time on a thread (a thread's window is the thread).
  • Hour is in the memory tier and not the lens; either add it to the lens or drop it — one list.
Proposal F

Deep links: every comment has an address

The share glyph on a comment (D, bottom-right, quiet) is only worth having if what it copies lands somewhere. Today it would not: the memory tier's continue-stub writes ?focus=<id> but threadView never reads it, and on the lens /p?uri= is built for a post — a board row's “replying to” link opens the parent, and the substrate fetches getPostThread(uri, depth 10) with nothing rendered above the head, so a reply's own uri would open it as an orphan root.

The address

  • Memory: /f/<slug>/p/<post>?focus=<comment> — the shape already in the stub; make the view honour it.
  • Lens: /p?uri=<post at://>&focus=<reply at://>; and a bare /p?uri=<reply at://> resolves to the same page by walking reply.root from the record.
  • Share copies this; the ⋯ menu's “Copy link” is the same link, and “Open on bsky.app” opens the reply there.

Landing (proposal: Reddit's shape)

  • The whole thread renders; the page scrolls to the focused comment and tints it briefly (the --tint token, fading over ~2s).
  • Its ancestor chain is expanded whatever the collapse state; siblings collapse to the ⊕ “n more” form so the focused branch is what you see first.
  • A one-line bar above it: “Viewing one comment — see the whole thread”, as Reddit does, so nobody thinks the thread is three replies long.

Alternative: bsky.app's shape

  • The reply becomes the head of its own page, with its parent (and grandparent) drawn above it, greyed, connected by the same rail.
  • Cheaper on the lens — one getPostThread with parentHeight — but it makes “the thread” mean two different pages, and the forum framing prefers one.
