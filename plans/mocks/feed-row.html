The feed row, made to agree with the phone

One proposal in five decisions, for one purpose: the lens feed row on forage.fyi should look the way the owner's phone expected it to, with Bluesky's own row as the reference — and this time the thing being agreed on is the app, not a drawing. Origin: six phone screenshots of forage.fyi and bsky.app taken 2026-08-30 16:17–16:28 (phpBB skin, f/whats-hot and two threads), plus a desktop pair (/u/pds.ls → f/pds.ls → "Unknown feed"). They showed: the picture the thread page renders missing from the feed; a post's text set as a bold heading; and an action row whose share arrow wrapped under "7315 · 270 comments · 1225", with the dashed like box two lines tall beside it. None of it was visible in board-cards v9's captures — default skin, card density, "1284 · 40 comments · 12".

Every frame on this page is a capture of the engine (MOCKS.md P1): Current is main at fe2b77b, Proposed is claude/feed-card at 8f25520, both by scripts/mock-snaps.mjs against e2e/harness/mock-board.mjs / mock-thread.mjs, in the default skin (rule 4 — the phone's phpBB skin restyles and restructures nothing, so it is not on this page), at 390 × 844 and 1280 × 900. The population now carries the phone's load (P2): a row with 7315 likes, 270 replies and 1225 reposts, and a four-line text-plus-picture post. Nothing here is drawn; there is no sketch to fall short of.
