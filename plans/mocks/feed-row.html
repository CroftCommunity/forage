The feed row, made to agree with the phone

One proposal in twenty-six decisions (five new since v9: 22–26), for one purpose: the lens feed row on forage.fyi should look the way the owner's phone expected it to, with Bluesky's own row as the reference — and this time the thing being agreed on is the app, not a drawing. Origin: six phone screenshots of forage.fyi and bsky.app taken 2026-08-30 16:17–16:28 (phpBB skin, f/whats-hot and two threads), plus a desktop pair (/u/pds.ls → f/pds.ls → "Unknown feed"). They showed: the picture the thread page renders missing from the feed; a post's text set as a bold heading; and an action row whose share arrow wrapped under "7315 · 270 comments · 1225", with the dashed like box two lines tall beside it. None of it was visible in board-cards v9's captures — default skin, card density, "1284 · 40 comments · 12".

Every frame on this page is a capture of the engine (MOCKS.md P1): Current is main at ad29be5 (v1–v9 landed there; v10 measures against it), Proposed is claude/feed-row-hover at 270bd10 (plan 2026-08-30-plan-feed-row-pointer-reply-quotes), both by scripts/mock-snaps.mjs against e2e/harness/mock-board.mjs / mock-thread.mjs, in the default skin (rule 4) — and, from v4 at the owner's ask, § H shows the board, the thread and the reply page in three skins (Forage light, Forage dark, phpBB — the phone's), at 390 × 844 and 1280 × 900. The population now carries the phone's load (P2): a row with 7315 likes, 270 replies and 1225 reposts, a four-line text-plus-picture post, and (v2) the names people choose at the widths the network allows — a 64-grapheme name, an emoji name, no name, and one that impersonates. Nothing here is drawn; there is no sketch to fall short of.
