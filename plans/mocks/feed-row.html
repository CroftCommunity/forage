The feed row, made to agree with the phone

One proposal in nineteen decisions, for one purpose: the lens feed row on forage.fyi should look the way the owner's phone expected it to, with Bluesky's own row as the reference — and this time the thing being agreed on is the app, not a drawing. Origin: six phone screenshots of forage.fyi and bsky.app taken 2026-08-30 16:17–16:28 (phpBB skin, f/whats-hot and two threads), plus a desktop pair (/u/pds.ls → f/pds.ls → "Unknown feed"). They showed: the picture the thread page renders missing from the feed; a post's text set as a bold heading; and an action row whose share arrow wrapped under "7315 · 270 comments · 1225", with the dashed like box two lines tall beside it. None of it was visible in board-cards v9's captures — default skin, card density, "1284 · 40 comments · 12".

Every frame on this page is a capture of the engine (MOCKS.md P1): Current is main at 7c4ee03 (v1–v5 landed there 2026-08-30 night; v6 measures against it), Proposed is claude/thread-head at 6abf8dd, both by scripts/mock-snaps.mjs against e2e/harness/mock-board.mjs / mock-thread.mjs, in the default skin (rule 4) — and, from v4 at the owner's ask, § H shows the board, the thread and the reply page in three skins (Forage light, Forage dark, phpBB — the phone's), at 390 × 844 and 1280 × 900. The population now carries the phone's load (P2): a row with 7315 likes, 270 replies and 1225 reposts, a four-line text-plus-picture post, and (v2) the names people choose at the widths the network allows — a 64-grapheme name, an emoji name, no name, and one that impersonates. Nothing here is drawn; there is no sketch to fall short of.

Open — not built, the owner's call

  • O0 — the handle's visibility (decision 6's alternative). As built, an impersonating name is caught only by hovering. Recommend: the muted handle after the name, at least on the row — it costs one line nothing on desktop and ellipsises on a phone; say the word and v3 captures it.
  • O7 — images on a reply. Closed in v6 (decision 16): the page carries the composer's picker; the quick box stays text.
  • O9 — GIF search. bsky.app searches Tenor through its own proxy; the endpoint and key are unverified from here (CLAUDE.md § External APIs: probe first). Until then the GIF button attaches a .gif from the device. Recommend: a probe, then a picker in a later revision.
  • O8 — a native mirror of drafts. Push the words to app.bsky.draft.* as well, keeping the parent locally (decision 11's finding). Wants a live probe with the standing test account before any code. Recommend: after the local stash has been used for a week.
  • O1 — line icons for the four. Today 💬 is a coloured emoji, ⟳ a thin text glyph, and the person an SVG: three weights in one row. Bluesky's are four line icons of one weight. Recommend: yes, next revision — it is a glyph swap, no layout change.
  • O2 — the thread head's like is still boxed (▲ 11 in a pill) while the row's is not. Recommend: the head follows the row.
  • O3 — should the phpBB family still prefer compact now that compact keeps the picture? Recommend: yes — it is only density now, which is what a skin was allowed to suggest.
  • O4 — the f/whats-hot breadcrumb line. Closed in v7 (decision 18): gone from lens rows.
  • O10 — the ☆ beside Join. Two account flags (saved vs pinned to the tab row; the star is a superset). Recommend: one Join that saves and pins, Unpin in the feed's ⋯ — it changes what Join writes to the account, so it waits for the owner's word.
  • O5 — the desktop pair's OTHER finding: that Brave tab showed the retired media slider and the pre-branch-A toolbar — a shell from before 2026-08-29. forage.fyi serves forage-v62 (main). The service worker installs with skipWaiting + clients.claim, but a long-lived SPA tab only picks the new shell up on a reload. Nothing to build; reload that tab. Whether the app should say "a newer Forage is ready — reload" is a separate question.
